🪂 QUANTUM ROYALE
You are Q-Bot — the robot from Space Shooter — in the blue circle.
23 sticker pups drop in with you. Everyone starts with nothing: roam the galaxy arena and grab the four colored weapons — blue regular, red rapid-fire, green shotgun, yellow rockets. You can carry one of each — tap the weapon display at the top or the weapon button (or press Q / 1–4) to switch between them.
Stack shields and heals, and use the blocks for cover. The storm keeps shrinking and burns harder every stage — stay in the circle.
Touch: the thumbsticks live in the bottom corners of the arena — left to move, right to aim & fire. They're always on screen.
Desktop: WASD / arrows to move, mouse to aim, click & hold to shoot.
Last fighter standing wins. Score = KOs + placement, with a big bonus for the win.

How to play: you drop in unarmed, so your first job is loot — walk over a weapon to collect it. There are four weapon colors and you can carry one of each: blue is the regular blaster, red is rapid-fire, green is a spread shotgun, and yellow fires explosive rockets. Tap the weapon display at the top of the screen, or the weapon button at the bottom of the arena (or press Q / 1–4), to switch between the weapons you've collected. Shields soak damage before your health, and heals patch you up. The storm circle shrinks in stages and burns more each stage, so keep an eye on the timer and stay inside it. On touch screens the thumbsticks stay fixed in the bottom corners of the arena — left to move, right to aim and fire. Blocks are solid cover — duck behind them to break line of sight. Every rival face comes from the full 106-sticker Quantum Inu pack.
