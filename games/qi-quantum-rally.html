✦ New Best! ✦

How to play: drag left or right anywhere on the road and the lowrider follows your finger, leaning into hard steering. Traffic cruises in your direction but slower — overtake it. Skim past with inches to spare for a +25 near-miss, and chain them to build your combo multiplier. Golden atoms on the asphalt are worth +10 each. Every crash bends a fender and scrubs your speed; lose all 3 and the run ends. Trucks are long and slow and swallow a whole lane; motorcycles are quick and drift side to side as they ride, so give them room — or thread them for a near-miss. The longer you survive, the higher your level climbs: top speed keeps rising past the old cap, traffic spawns thicker, and trucks and bikes make up more of the mix.
